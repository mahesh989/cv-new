Impact Data Analyst Social Traders Job Summary Social Traders Applications close: Job posted on: 28th Oct 2025 Melbourne > CBD & Inner Suburbs Melbourne Full Time Information Technology & Digital Not For Profit (NFP) Job description Permanent full-time Melbourne (preferred) Be part of a purpose-driven organisation creating real social impact. Work with Australia’s most comprehensive social enterprise dataset. Hybrid working environment and supportive team culture. Are you passionate about using data to drive social change? Social Traders is Australia’s leading organisation dedicated to building a thriving social enterprise sector. We certify social enterprises, connect them with business and government buyers, and advocate for social procurement policy. We’re looking for an Impact Data Analyst to join our Insights & Impact team. In this role, you’ll help bring Australia’s deepest social enterprise dataset to life—tracking and reporting on the impact of social procurement, supporting evidence-based advocacy, and contributing to thought leadership that influences policy and funding for the sector. What you’ll do Maintain and enhance our impact data framework and reporting systems. Analyse and report on social performance and social procurement outcomes for members, government, and funders. Contribute to Social Traders’ publications, including impact reports and sector insights. Collaborate across teams to define new data collection processes and measure outcomes. Support evidence for advocacy and funding proposals. What we’re looking for Strong analytical skills and experience in data analysis and reporting. Familiarity with data management tools (e.g., Salesforce, Power BI). Experience in social impact measurement or Theory of Change (highly regarded). Ability to communicate insights clearly to diverse stakeholders. A passion for social change and alignment with Social Traders’ mission. About us Social Traders are the certifier of social enterprises in Australia, a sector advocate, and the trailblazers of social enterprise procurement in Australia. We aspire to create a culture that is diverse, optimistic, innovative, and collaborative. We champion diversity and inclusivity in our team, starting with imagination and talking openly with curiosity. We take an ecosystem perspective and work as an intermediary to connect the dots between social enterprises and our corporate members. We actively support Reconciliation and the strengthening of relationships between Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples and non-Indigenous Australians. Apply to join, telling us why this opportunity is for you! We encourage individuals from all stages, backgrounds, and life experiences to join our team. First Nations People, people living with disability, people experiencing mental health conditions, parents, carers, and individuals across a range of ethnicities, religious beliefs, and gender and/or sexual orientations are encouraged to apply . We offer flexible working arrangements , ongoing support for professional development , and our employer-funded Employee Access Program (EAP) for you and your immediate family.
